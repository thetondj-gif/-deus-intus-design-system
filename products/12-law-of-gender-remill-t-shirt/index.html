The piece

Meaning before merchandise.

The Law of GenderThe Deus Intus Law of Gender Remill T-Shirt is part of our 12 Universal Laws Collection. This design represents the principle of balancing the forces within. The back artwork features a gold winged caduceus-inspired symbol with a radiant sun, mirrored wings, and a serpent-like form rising around the central staff. Beneath the artwork is the message “Balance The Forces Within.”The Law of Gender, within the Deus Intus philosophy, is about harmony between complementary inner energies. It speaks to receptivity and direction, intuition and structure, softness and strength, yielding and initiating. This piece is not about labels or limitation. It is a wearable reminder that creation becomes more powerful when the inner forces are integrated rather than divided. The soft pink garment gives the design warmth and openness, while the gold artwork brings a premium sacred finish. Designed in the Deus Intus sacred-modern style, this T-shirt is reflective, symbolic, and easy to wear. Printed on a Remill-style recycled cotton T-shirt, this piece carries both visual meaning and material intention. Premium spiritual streetwear style. Designed for reflective everyday wear. Care: Wash inside out with similar colours at 30°C. Do not bleach. Do not tumble dry. Do not iron directly over the print.
