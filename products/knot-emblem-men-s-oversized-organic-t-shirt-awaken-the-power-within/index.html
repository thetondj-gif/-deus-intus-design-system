The piece

Meaning before merchandise.

Awaken the Power Within. The Deus Intus Knot Emblem Men’s Oversized Tee is a premium spiritual streetwear piece created for those who value inner power, conscious discipline, and clean elevated style. Made from certified organic cotton with a heavier-weight 230 gsm fabric, this oversized t-shirt delivers a premium feel with a relaxed, on-trend silhouette. The dropped shoulders and loose fit give it a modern streetwear shape while keeping it comfortable enough for everyday wear. The chest features the Deus Intus sacred knot emblem in gold and ember tones, creating a bold yet refined 3D-effect finish against the deep black fabric. The design represents connection, movement, inner strength, and the eternal power that already exists within. This is a piece for those who do not need to shout to be seen. Wear it as a reminder of self-mastery, discipline, alignment, and quiet confidence. Designed to be remade, this t-shirt includes a QR code on the care label so it can be sent back when worn out, supporting a more circular and conscious approach to fashion.
