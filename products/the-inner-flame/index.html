The piece

Meaning before merchandise.

The fire was never outside you. It was always here. Waiting to be commanded. The Inner Flame tee carries the central truth of Deus Intus — that the divine power every person searches for externally already burns within them. Not as metaphor. As fact. Wear it as remembrance. Wear it as activation. About this piece: A bold back graphic commands the full attention of this piece. The sacred flame rises from within a golden triangle — the ancient symbol of ascension, fire, and divine will — framed by radiant sacred geometry and a ceremonial lotus base. Above it, the declaration: THE INNER FLAME. Below it, the source: DEUS INTUS — Awaken the Power Within. Gold, amber, and sacred earth tones on deep black. Rich, ceremonial, and unmistakably intentional. This is not a graphic tee. This is a statement of inner sovereignty. Fabric and fit: Circular economy organic cotton — 160 gsm Crafted from 50% post-consumer remanufactured organic cotton and 50% certified organic cotton Classic fit — clean, versatile, and built to last Soft, breathable, and light enough for everyday wear GM-free — not tested on animals Contains no animal-derived products Wash cool, hang dry — preserve the print and the planet Made to be remade. This tee already carries a second life within it — half of its fabric was rescued, reprocessed, and reborn from post-consumer organic cotton.
