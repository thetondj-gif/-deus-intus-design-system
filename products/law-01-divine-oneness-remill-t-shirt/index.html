The piece

Meaning before merchandise.

The Law 01 Divine Oneness Remill T-Shirt is the opening piece in the Deus Intus 12 Universal Laws Collection. Designed as a wearable symbol of connection, responsibility, and inner remembrance, this piece reflects the first universal law: everything is connected. Your energy, choices, thoughts, words, and presence ripple beyond the self. The artwork follows the Deus Intus visual world: sacred-modern, calm, symbolic, and premium — using refined geometry, spiritual clarity, and restrained visual power rather than loud decoration. Deus Intus is built around the belief that the power people seek externally already exists within them, and this first law begins the collection by grounding that truth in interconnectedness. This tee is printed on a Remill-style recycled cotton T-shirt, chosen for its softer impact and conscious material story. The Remill T-shirt is listed as made from 50% post-consumer remanufactured organic cotton and 50% organic cotton, with a 160gsm fabric weight.
