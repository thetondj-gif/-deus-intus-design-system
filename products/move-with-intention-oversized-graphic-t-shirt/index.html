The piece

Meaning before merchandise.

The Move With Intention Oversized Tee is built for the person choosing presence over autopilot. This design carries one of the most practical commands in the Deus Intus world: move with intention. Not with panic. Not with performance. Not with the pressure to prove yourself. But with clarity, awareness, and inner alignment. The back artwork features bold stacked typography framed by a circular compass motif, giving the piece a sense of direction, movement, and centred self-leadership. The neon blue, violet, and magenta gradient adds luminous energy while the clean composition keeps the design grounded and wearable. Unlike louder spiritual graphics, this piece is direct, minimal, and command-led. It does not over-explain. It simply reminds the wearer to bring consciousness into the next step. Cut for an oversized fit, this tee works as an everyday statement piece within the INNR COMMAND collection. The grey garment base gives the artwork a softer, streetwear-ready contrast, making it ideal for customers who want the Deus Intus message in a cleaner, more versatile colourway. This is for the ones learning that alignment is not only something you feel. It is something you practice. It is something you move with. Design StoryThis tee turns intentional action into a visual command. The circular frame represents centred awareness. The compass points symbolise direction, choice, and inner navigation. The stacked typography creates strength and clarity.
