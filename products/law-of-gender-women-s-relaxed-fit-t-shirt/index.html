The piece

Meaning before merchandise.

The Law of Gender Women’s Relaxed Fit T-Shirt is a wearable reminder of inner balance, creative power, and embodied alignment. In the Deus Intus interpretation of the 12 Universal Laws, the Law of Gender speaks to the balance of internal energies: stillness and action, intuition and structure, receiving and creating. This piece was designed for women who move through life with awareness, softness, discipline, and quiet strength. Featuring a gold symbolic design on a relaxed silhouette, this t-shirt carries the phrase: Balance the Forces Within. Made from remanufactured organic cotton through the Remill® circular process, the garment reflects the same principle it represents: transformation, renewal, and conscious responsibility. Material is recovered, shredded, respun, and made to be remade again when worn out. Wear it loose, tucked, or tied at the side. Let it be more than clothing. Let it be a daily return to centre.
