The piece

Meaning before merchandise.

Command Your Interior. The Sovereign Flame Tee is more than a silhouette; it is a uniform for internal governance. Designed with a versatile, relaxed fit, this piece features side-split detailing and rolled sleeves, allowing you to adapt your look to your environment—tied for focus, tucked for structure, or loose for flow. Crafted from 155gsm certified organic cotton, the fabric offers a lightweight yet grounded feel against the skin. Featuring the Deus Intus central sigil, this garment serves as a physical anchor for emotional command and nervous system sovereignty. Built for the Cycle. True mastery respects the Law of Cause and Effect. This tee is circular by design. When it has served its purpose, scan the QR code on the care label to return it to us. We will remake it into new yarn, ensuring nothing is wasted.

Key features

Signature Sacred Design: High-detail sacred geometry with the "Awaken the Power Within" command. Versatile Architecture: Split sides and rolled sleeves for multiple styling expressions. Ethical Foundation: 155gsm certified organic cotton, GM-free, and PETA-approved vegan. Renewable Energy Crafted: Produced in a factory powered by wind and solar energy. Circular Lifecycle: Designed to be remade; returnable via integrated QR technology. Care Instruction: Wash cool. Hang dry. Align with intent.
