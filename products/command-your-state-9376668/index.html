The piece

Meaning before merchandise.

The work is inward. The results are lived. The Command Your State hoodie is for the woman who has chosen herself — deliberately, repeatedly, without apology. A piece built for the long practice. Worn in morning rituals, late resets, and every quiet moment of return. The Deus Intus sacred flame seal at the chest. The declaration on the back. Gold on white — clear, grounded, and sovereign. Wear what you stand for. About this piece: A premium circular economy hoodie designed for conscious, everyday wear. Heavyweight, soft, and purposefully constructed — with subtle side splits that give it an elevated, relaxed silhouette. This is the piece you live in. The sacred flame seal sits centred on the chest. The Command Your State graphic anchors the back. Both rendered in sacred gold on white — minimal, powerful, unmistakably Deus Intus. Fabric and fit: Circular economy organic cotton — 340 gsm Crafted from 50% post-consumer remanufactured organic cotton and 50% certified organic cotton Subtle side splits for a modern, elevated fit Heavyweight and warm without feeling heavy GM-free — not tested on animals Contains no animal-derived products Wash cool, hang dry — to preserve print quality and shape Made to be remade. This hoodie is part of a closed-loop system. Half of it already existed — remade from post-consumer organic cotton, given a second life as something intentional.
