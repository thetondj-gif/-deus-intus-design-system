The piece

Meaning before merchandise.

Every action echoes. Choose with awareness. The Law of Cause & Effect teaches that nothing exists in isolation. Every thought, choice, habit, word, and action creates movement. What we repeat becomes a pattern. What we practise becomes a path. This Deus Intus design is the fifth release in the 12 Universal Laws Series, created as a wearable reminder that your life is shaped through conscious cause, not passive waiting. Alignment is not only what you believe. It is what you choose, repeat, repair, and embody. Printed in radiant gold on a soft grey circular economy t-shirt, the artwork features expanding ripples, sacred geometry, radiant linework, and a central point of impact to symbolise consequence, accountability, energetic movement, and the unseen echo of every action. It is made from material that has been recovered, shredded, and respun through an innovative process called Remill®, saving it from landfill and giving it a new life. A unique neck label printed onto the garment calls out its circular nature, and the care label includes a QR code so the t-shirt can be sent back again when it is worn out. Designed to be remade. Created to remind you. Made for those learning to move with intention before the echo returns.

The principle behind the piece

The Law of Cause and Effect.

This design is a visual anchor for one principle within the complete 12 Universal Laws system. Explore its meaning, symbolism and grounded practice, then return to the full women’s series.
