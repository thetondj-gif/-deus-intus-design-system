The piece

Meaning before merchandise.

Order is not imposed. It is remembered. The Alignment is a sacred geometry wall sculpture of extraordinary precision — a gold wire star mandala, layered and interwoven in eight-pointed symmetry, suspended within a deep matte obsidian ring. Framed in clean matte black against a textured off-white linen ground. Four points. Eight directions. One centre. Everything in its place. Everything in relationship. ✦ THE ARTWORKWhere The Emanation radiates outward, The Alignment draws inward — toward a hollow centre that holds more than any solid form could. The eight-pointed star geometry speaks to the ancient principle of as above, so below: perfect correspondence between the inner and outer, between what is seen and what governs. The gold wire mandala is built from intersecting triangles, overlapping circles, and spiralling arcs — creating a living, breathing pattern that shifts and reveals itself depending on the light and the angle you bring to it. The matte black frame and obsidian ring create a contained, monastic field around the piece. Nothing competes. Nothing distracts. The mandala holds the full attention of the space. ✦ THE OBJECTThis is a three-dimensional sculptural wall piece — not a flat print. The gold wire mandala element has physical depth and intricate layering, catching light differently at morning, midday, and evening. No two viewings are identical. Every piece is individually crafted.
