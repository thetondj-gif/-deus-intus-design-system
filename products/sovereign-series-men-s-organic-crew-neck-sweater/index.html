The piece

Meaning before merchandise.

The Sovereign Series Men’s Crew Neck Sweater is designed for those who move with quiet strength. Made from certified organic cotton with a soft 300 gsm feel, this classic crew neck combines everyday comfort with elevated symbolic design. The front artwork features a radiant gold Deus Intus emblem: flame, crown, geometry, and circular alignment — a visual mark of inner power, self-mastery, and conscious discipline. Set against a deep blue base, the design carries a refined sacred-modern aesthetic without excess. It is bold enough to stand alone, yet restrained enough for daily wear. This sweater is part of the Deus Intus wearable philosophy: clothing that feels intentional, grounded, and built around the belief that power is not found outside you — it is remembered within. Designed to be remade, each piece includes a QR code on the care label so it can be sent back when worn out and returned into the cycle.

Key features

Men’s classic crew neck sweater. Certified organic cotton sweatshirt. Soft 300 gsm fabric. Sacred gold Sovereign Series front emblem. Deep blue premium base colour. GM-free and vegan-friendly. Not tested on animals. Made in a renewable energy-powered factory. Audited for social and sustainability criteria. Designed to be remade through QR code return label. Wash cool, hang dry.

Design meaning

The central flame represents the power within. The crown symbolises sovereignty and self-command.
