The piece

Meaning before merchandise.

All is one. Nothing exists in isolation. The Law of Divine Oneness teaches that everything is connected. Your thoughts, emotions, choices, words, relationships, and actions all form part of a greater field of life. What you heal within yourself does not end with you. It changes how you move, how you relate, and what you contribute to the world around you. This Deus Intus design is the twelfth release in the 12 Universal Laws Series, created as a wearable reminder that inner work is never separate from outer impact. The more consciously you return to yourself, the more consciously you meet everything else. Printed in radiant gold on a soft rose-toned circular economy t-shirt, the artwork features a meditative human form, connected light points, sacred geometry, and radiant network lines to symbolise unity, inner connection, collective energy, consciousness, and the divine thread running through all life. It is made from material that has been recovered, shredded, and respun through an innovative process called Remill®, saving it from landfill and giving it a new life. A unique neck label printed onto the garment calls out its circular nature, and the care label includes a QR code so the t-shirt can be sent back again when it is worn out. Designed to be remade. Created to remind you. Made for those learning to live from connection, not separation.

The principle behind the piece

The Law of Divine Oneness.

This design is a visual anchor for one principle within the complete 12 Universal Laws system. Explore its meaning, symbolism and grounded practice, then return to the full women’s series.
