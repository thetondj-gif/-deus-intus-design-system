The piece

Meaning before merchandise.

The Sacred Flame Core T-Shirt Bundle brings together three essential colourways: light blue, deep black, and clean white. Each t-shirt features the Deus Intus Sacred Flame emblem placed subtly on the chest — a refined symbol of inner power, alignment, and self-command. The design is intentionally minimal, giving the shirts a premium everyday feel rather than a loud graphic look. This bundle is built as a clean foundation for conscious dressing. The white tee offers clarity and simplicity. The black tee brings depth and grounded presence. The light blue tee adds softness, breath, and calm renewal. Designed for everyday wear, layering, slow mornings, studio work, meditation, journaling, travel, and relaxed weekends, this set gives you a versatile rotation of symbolic essentials. Design meaning

The Sacred Flame emblem is a quiet mark of inner authority. The flame represents the power within. The triangle reflects discipline, focus, and upward movement. The circular seal symbolises wholeness, rhythm, and return to centre.
