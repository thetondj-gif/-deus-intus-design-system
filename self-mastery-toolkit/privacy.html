Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 July 2026

What we collect

The Deus Intus Self-Mastery Toolkit runs in your browser. By default, everything you enter — your morning sequence, boundary card, weekly review scores — is stored only on your own device using the browser's local storage. It is never uploaded unless you explicitly choose to.

Email opt-in

If you submit your email on a tool's opt-in form, we send it (with your consent) to our email provider so we can deliver the sequence or reminder you requested. We store only your email address and the source tool. We do not sell or share your email with third parties for marketing.

You can withdraw consent at any time by contacting support@deusintus.com or by using the "Clear my saved data" link in any tool.

Analytics

We collect anonymous, aggregated usage events (e.g. which tool was opened, completed, or shared) to improve the product. These events contain no personally identifying content from your entries. You can disable analytics by blocking scripts or by clearing your local data.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data we hold about you. To exercise these rights, email support@deusintus.com.

Data we do not collect

We do not collect special-category (health, religious, or philosophical) data with the intent of profiling you. The Toolkit supports reflection and discipline; it is not therapy, medical, or psychological treatment.
