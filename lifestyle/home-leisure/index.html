What is included in Home & Leisure?

Relaxed T-shirts, hoodies, sweatshirts, cropped layers and selected wall art for home and workspace settings.

Are these pyjamas?

No. They are premium casual and leisure pieces suitable for home, errands, travel and relaxed everyday wear.

How does wall art fit the edit?

The wall art extends the same symbolic language into the environment, creating a cohesive home or workspace story.
