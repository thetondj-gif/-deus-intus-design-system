Questions

Before you choose.

Is this technical yoga clothing?

Not unless a product explicitly states technical fabric properties. The current edit focuses on relaxed apparel and studio layers.

Which messages suit mindful practice?

Return to Centre, Rhythm, Vibration and Command Your State are especially aligned with breath, awareness and reflection.

Can I wear these outside the studio?

Yes. The edit is designed to transition between mindful practice, home and everyday casual wear.
