12 Universal Laws

The Laws, Made Wearable

A symbolic clothing series inspired by vibration, rhythm, cause, action, polarity, correspondence and conscious responsibility.

The collection concept

Each design translates one principle into a wearable reminder. The collection is not a promise of guaranteed outcomes; it is an invitation to practise awareness, responsibility and deliberate action.

The design inspiration

Twelve principles. Twelve visual anchors.

Each piece begins with a principle, then translates it into a restrained sacred-modern symbol. The artwork is a prompt for reflection and practice—not a promise that wearing it will produce an outcome.

The collection in practice

A system of reflection—not a promise

01 / The idea

The collection treats the Universal Laws as twelve lenses through which to examine attention, conduct, consequence, cycles and relationship. Each design has its own visual language, while the gold linework and centred compositions hold the capsule together as one system.

02 / The expression

The series moves from connection and vibration through correspondence, attraction and action, then into consequence, perspective, polarity, rhythm and complementary creative forces. It is designed to be collected by principle or worn as the reminder most relevant to the season you are in.

Choose the principle that challenges your current pattern—not merely the one that confirms it.

Questions

Before you choose.

Do you offer all 12 Laws?

The collection includes the full men’s Law series and an expanding women’s range, grouped by principle and fit.

What do the designs represent?

Each piece interprets one Universal Law through symbolic artwork and a concise principle intended to support reflection and daily practice.

Are these manifestation guarantees?

No. Deus Intus does not promise guaranteed results. The collection focuses on awareness, conduct and personal responsibility.
